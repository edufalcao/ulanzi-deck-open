Editing tips

Fast ways to build a page.

  • Click a key to edit it.
  • Drag any image onto a key to use it as the icon (scaled to 196×196).
  • Select a key and press ⌘C / ⌘V to duplicate, Delete to clear (⌘Z undoes).
  • Drag a page in the sidebar to reorder it, or press ⌥↑ / ⌥↓.
  • In Page settings, New subpage creates a child page plus the keys to open and leave it.
  • Press ⌘S to push the page to the Deck.
  • Pinned keys appear on every page of the profile.
